# ServerSideTweaks

**server-side mod** - only the host needs it installed.

A small list of server-side configurable tweaks.

[![ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/img/githubbutton_sm.svg)](https://ko-fi.com/E1E71PHUJ2)

## General Tweaks

- Lunar items can turn into a Pearl or an Irradiant Pearl, except in the Bazaar.
- Children teleport a shorter distance and are no longer invincible while teleporting.
- With the Artifact of Evolution, Void Jailers, Solus Invalidators and Voidling get no Alien Heads.
- The combat director can lean towards many weak enemies or a few strong ones, separately for normal runs and Simulacrum.

## Simulacrum Tweaks

- A player who took more than their share of the wave rewards has to wait for the others. Skull Tokens show how many rewards each player can still take.
- (ShareSuite only) Wave rewards aren't shared.
- In Commencement, each wave has a chance to turn on the Artifact of Dissonance for more enemy variety.
- Set the most enemies at once and how long a wave lasts.
- The Void Crab can get faster with every wave.
- While a wave has no enemies alive, the next ones come sooner.
- Captains get their beacons back after each boss wave.
- Broken drones can appear, as on normal stages.
